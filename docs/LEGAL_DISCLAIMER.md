## 1. Decentralized & Neutral Protocol Architecture

**RenegadeSwarm** is an open-source, client-side, peer-to-peer (P2P) file transfer protocol and desktop client engineered exclusively for decentralized distribution of machine learning weights and AI model architectures (such as SafeTensors, GGUF, and ONNX formats).

1. **No Centralized Servers or Hosting**: The developers, maintainers, and contributors of RenegadeSwarm **do not host, store, cache, transmit, index, or curate** any AI models, weights, datasets, or user files.
2. **Decentralized Operation**: All swarms, transfers, and metadata distribution occur directly between autonomous end-user nodes via the Mainline BitTorrent Distributed Hash Table (DHT) and user-specified Web Seeds.
3. **No Editorial or Administrative Control**: The creators and contributors have no technical ability, editorial oversight, administrative access, or supervisory capability to monitor, censor, intercept, modify, or terminate any data transferred across the decentralized network.
